Our story

A passion for vintage, built into wholesale

Photo of Helena & Dieter

What started as a shared passion for vintage clothing and the thrill of finding great pieces eventually grew into Vintage Wholesale Belgium.

We're Dieter & Helena, the people behind Vintage Wholesale Belgium. Over the years, our love for sourcing, collecting and trading vintage turned into what we are today: a dedicated vintage wholesaler supplying stores and resellers all over the world.

At our warehouse, it's all about the freedom to explore. Our customers come in, go through the stock and create their own selection, piece by piece, choosing exactly what works for their store and their customers. And when you're looking for something more specific, we listen.

Every store has its own identity, aesthetic and audience. Share your ideas with us, show us your moodboards, tell us what's working and what you're looking for. Based on that, we can prepare a more tailored selection for you to explore.

For us, wholesale isn't about telling you what you should buy. You know your store best. We're here to provide the stock, the variety and the experience to help you find what feels right.

We love meeting the people behind the stores, discovering different concepts and seeing how vintage is interpreted in completely different ways around the world.

What started as a passion became our business, but the feeling never really changed. We still love the hunt. We still love finding great vintage. And we love seeing where it ends up next.

Dieter & Helena
